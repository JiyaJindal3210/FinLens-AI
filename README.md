## 🌟 Overview

FinLens AI is an intelligent financial companion that helps users understand **why** they spend, not just **where** they spend.

Unlike traditional expense trackers, FinLens AI combines artificial intelligence, behavioral analytics, and financial insights to identify spending patterns, detect risky transactions, forecast future expenses, and provide personalized recommendations for smarter financial decisions.

The goal is to make personal finance proactive instead of reactive.
